Become the Elden Lord

What is the best starting class for beginners?

Vagabond is widely considered the best starting class for beginners due to its high base Vigor and solid armor, giving you extra survivability during early encounters.

Samurai is another top pick, coming equipped with the Uchigatana for Hemorrhage (bleed) damage and a Longbow for reliable ranged options.

For players who prefer spellcasting over physical combat, Astrologer provides a strong foundation focused heavily on sorceries. The Astrologer is the premier starting class for players focused purely on Intelligence-based spellcasting and ranged magic combat.

If you want a versatile faith-based caster who can also handle close-quarters combat, Confessor is an excellent alternative. Starting with a solid balance of Physical stats and Faith, a high-guard shield, and access to both healing and stealth incantations right out of the gate, it offers a broad safety net while allowing room to experiment with hybrid paladin builds.

Which Keepsake should you select?

The Golden Seed is the overall best choice because it immediately grants an additional charge for your Sacred Flasks, boosting your HP or FP recovery right from the start.
