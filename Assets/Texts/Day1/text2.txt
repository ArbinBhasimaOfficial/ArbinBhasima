Beating a First Boss Tree Sentinel

How to Beat a First Boss Tree Sentinel
    The best strategy for defeating the Tree Sentinel early on is to skip him initially, unlock your Mount (Torrent), and return once you have leveled up your attributes and upgraded your primary weapon. When fighting him, rely on mounted combat to match his mobility, circle toward his shield side to avoid his heavier halberd swipes, and time your hits immediately after he finishes his heavy charging or jump attacks.

The simple steps to getting started
    Bypass the Boss: Sneak past or ride around the Tree Sentinel upon leaving the Stranded Graveyard to reach the Church of Elleh.

    Unlock Torrent: Rest at three open-world Sites of Grace to meet Melina and receive the Spectral Steed Whistle.

    Upgrade Gear & Stats: Invest your Runes into Vigor for extra health and upgrade your weapon at the Church of Elleh anvil.

    Engage in Mounted Combat: Summon Torrent during the fight to quickly close distance and hit-and-run his vulnerable side.

    Punish Ground Slams: Dodge or ride away when he leaps into the air, then move in quickly to land consecutive attacks while he recovers.
