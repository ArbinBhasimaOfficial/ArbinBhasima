What is the best starting class for beginners?

Vagabond is widely considered the best starting class for beginners due to its high base Vigor and solid armor, giving you extra survivability during early encounters.

Samurai is another top pick, coming equipped with the Uchigatana for Hemorrhage (bleed) damage and a Longbow for reliable ranged options.

For players who prefer spellcasting over physical combat, Astrologer provides a strong foundation focused heavily on sorceries.

Which Keepsake should you select?

The Golden Seed is the overall best choice because it immediately grants an additional charge for your Sacred Flasks, boosting your HP or FP recovery right from the start.

Reasons to get started

    Complete Playstyle Freedom: You can reallocate your stats via Rebirth after defeating Rennala Queen of the Full Moon, allowing you to experiment with different builds throughout your journey.

    Flexible Exploration: Unlocking areas early with Stonesword Keys gives you early access to valuable weapons, spirit ashes, and side dungeons.

    Diverse Character Templates: Starting visual templates like Numen or Draconian allow deep visual customization without locking you into specific stat penalties.

    Instant Early Boosts: Starting items like the Lands Between Rune provide 3,000 instant runes to kickstart your early attribute leveling or merchant purchases.
